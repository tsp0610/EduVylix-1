# Sum of Two Numbers

- **SkillHub section:** Practice
- **SkillHub problem ID:** `sum-of-two-numbers`
- **Date:** 2026-09-26

## Problem

You are given two integers `A` and `B`.

Calculate and print the sum of the two numbers.

### Example

If `A = 15` and `B = 27`, then:

```text
15 + 27 = 42

## Constraints

- `-10^18 <= A, B <= 10^18`

## Input format

The input contains two space-separated integers `A` and `B`.

## Output format

Print a single integer representing the sum of `A` and `B`.

## Examples

### Example 1

**Input**

```text
15 27
```

**Output**

```text
42
```

**Explanation**

The sum of `15` and `27` is `42`.
